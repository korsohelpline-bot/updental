About Updental Academy

Education built around one habit: showing up every Sunday.

We're not another certificate mill. Updental Academy is a small, focused academy that runs one live webinar a week — practical, specialist-led, and small enough that your questions actually get answered.

Why "Sunday"?

Weekdays belong to your chair-side hours. Sunday evening is when practising dentists actually have the headspace to learn — so that's the only day we teach. One session, one topic, one hour and a half. No sprawling multi-week courses to fall behind on.
